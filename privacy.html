Trip Tracker – Privacy policy

Trip Tracker is a trip log for cars with Android Automotive OS, with a companion phone app. This page explains what it reads, where that data goes and how to delete it. Last updated: 25 November 2026.

What the app reads

  • Location (GPS position, altitude, accuracy, speed, heading) while the car is being driven, including when the app is not open, so trips are recorded automatically.
  • Car data through the car's own interface: speed, battery power, battery energy and capacity, range, outside temperature, gear, ignition state, charging port and charging state, and the car's display units.

Where the data goes

Location and car data stay in the car. They are stored only in the app's own storage on the car's infotainment system. The app has no account of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no server; it does not send trips, locations or car data anywhere except where you choose below.

Only when you ask for it:

  • Export: you can save a trip as a GPX or CSV file in the car's Downloads folder, or open a download page that the app serves on the car's own Wi-Fi network (for example its hotspot) for 15 minutes, protected by a random code shown on the car's screen. What happens to exported files is up to you.
  • Backup to Google Drive (off until you add an account): everything the app recorded (trips with their routes, idle time, charges) is copied to a folder "Trip Tracker" in the Google Drive of each Google account you add, and read from there by the Trip Tracker phone app if you sign in to it with the same account. The app asks Google only for access to files it creates itself (not the rest of your Drive) and for the account's e-mail address, to show which account it is. The data goes from the car to your own Drive; the developer has no access to it. Remove an account in the app's settings to stop; delete the folder in Drive to remove the backup.
  • Map background (off by default): if you choose a map in Settings, the car downloads map images of the area you are looking at from OpenStreetMap's tile servers. Those servers see which map area is requested and the car's IP address, as with any website; they do not receive your trips or any car data.

Keeping and deleting data

Trips are kept until you delete them: one trip on its own screen, or all trips in Settings. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data. The app is excluded from Android's cloud backup.

The phone app

The phone app has no data of its own. When you sign in with a Google account, it downloads the backup the car made in that account's Drive and shows it on the phone. It asks Google for the same limited access as the car (its own files and the e-mail address). Removing the account in the app removes the downloaded copy's source; uninstalling the app deletes the copy on the phone.

Permissions

Location (including "all the time", for recording without opening the app), the car's speed and energy values, notifications (the quiet notice that recording is ready), and network access (only for the optional map and the local download page).

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact address shown on the app's Google Play page.
