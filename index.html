Trip Tracker

Every drive, recorded by itself. Route, energy, speed, elevation.

Trip Tracker is an app for cars with Android Automotive OS. It records the car whenever it is on, without being opened, and turns that into trips, idle time and charges. While parked you see each trip: the route colored by consumption, speed or elevation, charts over distance or time, energy used and regenerated, and totals per day, week, month and year. It also estimates the remaining range from your own driving.

  • Data stays in the car. No account, no tracking, no ads.
  • Optional backup to your own Google Drive, and a phone app that shows the same trips from that backup.
  • Export as GPX and CSV.
